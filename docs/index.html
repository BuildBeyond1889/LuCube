Lu Cube

A music wheel that sits on the edge of your screen. Drag a record off it and it becomes a 3D cube you can spin, with a different face on every side.

⬇ Windows PC⬇ Android phoneView on GitHub
Free and open source. Your own music works with no internet and no account.
The cube

Six faces. One spin away.

Turn the cube to whichever face you need and it settles upright. Every button works right on the face: play, skip, scrub, pick a playlist.

TransportPlay, skip, scrub, volume, crossfade and a sleep timer.
PlaylistEvery song in the playlist; tap one to play it.
LibraryAll your playlists. Each folder of music is one.
  • Sits on your desktop. No window: just the wheel on the edge of the screen and the cube floating over everything. Clicks anywhere else go straight through.
  • Lyrics, cover art and a visualiser make up the other three faces. Drop a .lrc file next to a song for synced lyrics.
  • Takes on the weather where you are. Rain streaks, snow, dusk light. It gets darker at night.
  • Comes with free music if you want it. One click downloads public-domain classical or Creative Commons lofi, credited.
Radio

The whole world's radio. Press R.

One key flips the whole cube from your music to live radio. A line of light sweeps across every face. Whatever you were listening to waits there, paused, until you flip back.

Passport

Collect the world.

Every country you listen to gets a stamp. Reach 25, 75, 150 and finally all 197 countries to earn a permanent banner under the volume bar. Each banner also unlocks a new skin for the cube.

  • Wanderer: 25 countries. Checker trim, steel bars.
  • Voyager: 75 countries. Dotted trim, blue bars.
  • Globetrotter: 150 countries. Striped trim, red bars.
  • Citizen of Earth: all 197. X trim, gold bars, holographic.

Phone ANDROID · OUT NOW

Same cube. In your pocket.

The same cube and the same faces, on Android now (iPhone later). It plays through the phone's own audio system, so the lock screen, headphones, car and Bluetooth controls all work.

  • ♫ Your music, stored on the phone and played offline, with a thumb wheel for playlists.
  • 📻 World radio, with a globe at the bottom that spins to wherever you scroll.
  • ◎ Remote for whatever another app is playing. Spotify, YouTube Music and podcasts show up on the cube, and its buttons control them.
  • Song Catcher, home-screen widget, Android Auto, sunrise alarm, even volume, data saver, sync with your PC, and cube skins you unlock with passport banners.

Get it

Free. No account.

Windows PC or laptop

The desktop cube

  1. Download the zip and unzip it anywhere: a drive, a folder, a USB stick.
  2. Put folders of songs in Music (each folder is a playlist), or run Get Classical Music.bat / Get Lofi Music.bat for free music.
  3. Run Lu Cube Desktop.bat for the floating cube, or Lu Cube.bat for a window.
⬇ Download for Windows

Windows 10 or 11. If Python isn't installed, Lu Cube downloads its own portable copy (about 11 MB); the desktop cube downloads Electron once (about 100 MB). Make Lu Cube Stick.bat puts it on a USB stick for a friend. Set Weather Town.bat sets your town.

Android phone

The pocket cube

  1. Download LuCube.apk on the phone.
  2. Open it. Android asks once to allow installs from your browser: allow it, then tap Install.
  3. Open Lu Cube and allow music access. Folders of songs on the phone become playlists.
⬇ Download for Android

Android 7 or newer, about 6 MB. New versions install over the old one and keep your settings. To copy songs from your PC, run Phone Sync.bat on the PC and tap ⚙ → Sync on the phone (same Wi-Fi). iPhone: later.
