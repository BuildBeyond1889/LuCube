The cube

Six faces. One spin away.

Turn the cube to whichever face you need and it settles upright. Every button works right on the face: play, skip, scrub, pick a playlist.

TransportPlay, skip, scrub, volume, crossfade and a sleep timer.
PlaylistEvery song in the playlist; tap one to play it.
LibraryAll your playlists. Each folder of music is one.
  • Sits on your desktop. No window: just the wheel on the edge of the screen and the cube floating over everything. Clicks anywhere else go straight through.
  • Cover art and a visualiser are on the other faces: the album art big and square, and the music drawn as a 3D landscape.
  • Takes on the weather where you are. Rain streaks, snow, dusk light. It gets darker at night.
  • Comes with free music if you want it. One click downloads public-domain classical or Creative Commons lofi, credited.

♥ Song Catcher

Heard it on the radio? Keep it.

Tap the heart while a station plays and Lu Cube catches the song, with the station and the country it came from. Open your catches any time and find each one in a tap.

SPOTIFYYT

Stations come from the free radio-browser.info directory. Radio needs the internet; your own music never does.

Passport

Wander the world.

Every country you listen to gets a stamp. Reach 25, 75, 150 and finally 197 countries to earn a permanent banner under the volume bar. Each banner also unlocks a new skin for the cube.

  • Wanderer: 25 countries. Checker trim, steel bars.
  • Voyager: 75 countries. Dotted trim, blue bars.
  • Globetrotter: 150 countries. Striped trim, red bars.
  • Citizen of Earth: 197 countries. X trim, gold bars, holographic.

Unfold PC

Bigger screen? Unfold it.

Pull the cube open and its faces lay out flat as one big window: the list on the left, the world on the right, the player in the middle.

  • Everything at once. Channels or favourites, the player, the world map and your caught songs, side by side.
  • Idle Pong plays itself above the player. Click it to join in.
  • The visualiser goes full screen with one click: the music, drawn as a landscape in 3D.
  • Done? Shatter it and the pieces fly back to the wheel.

The cube's top face is Add Music: pick songs from your phone or Drive, pick a whole folder, or open two free libraries. Everything lands on the cube as a playlist, ready to play offline.

Lu Blocks ANDROID

A puzzle on all six faces.

The block puzzle everyone's playing, wrapped round a cube. Drop pieces on the face in front of you, spin the whole cube with your thumb to find room, and keep the music going while you play.

  • Clear lines to charge the rings that run round the cube.
  • Charge a whole ring and it blasts, for the big points and the combo.
  • Spin freely: flick it and it turns with weight, then settles square on a face.
  • Opens from the cube itself, from the Add Music face, with a sweep of light.

Get it

Free. No account.

Windows PC or laptop

The desktop cube

  1. Download the zip and unzip it anywhere: a drive, a folder, a USB stick.
  2. Put folders of songs in Music (each folder is a playlist), or run Get Classical Music.bat / Get Lofi Music.bat for free music.
  3. Run Lu Cube Desktop.bat for the floating cube, or Lu Cube.bat for a window.
⬇ Download for Windows

Windows 10 or 11. If Python isn't installed, Lu Cube downloads its own portable copy (about 11 MB); the desktop cube downloads Electron once (about 100 MB). Make Lu Cube Stick.bat puts it on a USB stick for a friend. Set Weather Town.bat sets your town.

Android phone

The pocket cube

  1. Download LuCube.apk on the phone.
  2. Open it. Android asks once to allow installs from your browser: allow it, then tap Install.
  3. Open Lu Cube and allow music access. Folders of songs on the phone become playlists, or turn to the Add Music face for free music, audiobooks and podcasts.
⬇ Download for Android

Android 7 or newer, about 6 MB. New versions install over the old one and keep your settings. To copy songs from your PC, run Phone Sync.bat on the PC and tap ⚙ → Sync on the phone (same Wi-Fi). iPhone: use the web app.

iPhone or iPad

The web app

  1. On the iPhone or iPad, open buildbeyond1889.github.io/LuCube/app in Safari.
  2. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen → Add. Lu Cube gets its own icon and opens full screen.
  3. Tap ＋ ADD SONGS and pick songs from Files (iCloud Drive, On My iPhone, Downloads). Each album becomes a playlist.
Open Lu Cube for iPhone & iPad

iOS / iPadOS 16.4 or newer. No App Store, no account, nothing to sideload. iPhone gets the phone layout; iPad gets the full cube with the unfold window and Pong. Songs you add stay inside the app and play offline. Web-app limits Apple sets: music may pause when the screen locks, the volume follows the side buttons, the radio can't show what song is playing, the visualiser dances on its own rather than to the sound, and there's no remote mode, Android-Auto-style car view or PC sync.
