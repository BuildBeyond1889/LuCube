HOW TO PLAY

Six boards. One cube.

  1. Drag a block from the tray onto the face in front.
  2. Or tap a block to pick it, then tap the front face where you want it.
  3. A dragged block waits, glowing, where you drop it: tap it to set it down, drag it somewhere else, or tap away to put it back.
  4. Drag the cube, or one of its yellow corners, to spin it any way you like. Flick it and it keeps spinning, then settles square on the nearest face. Arrow keys work too.
  5. Fill a row or column on a face and it clears, like any block game.
  6. Every row and column also runs all the way round the cube, through four faces. A line you clear stays charged and turns yellow. Once a ring is charged on three faces it pulses: one more line sets it off.
  7. Charge the same line on all four faces and the whole ring blasts: everything in it goes, for a big bonus.
  8. Clear lines on back-to-back moves for a combo.
  9. The game ends when no block in the tray fits on any face.
